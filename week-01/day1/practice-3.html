Party Jollof Rice

Serves 6 · 45 minutes · Medium

Spicy One pot

The smoky, tomato-rich rice that starts arguments at every family gathering. This is the party version, cooked long enough to catch at the bottom.

Ingredients

  • 3 cups long-grain parboiled rice
  • 6 large tomatoes, blended
  • 2 red bell peppers, diced
  • 1/2 cup vegetable oil
  • 3 tablespoons tomato paste
  • 2 stock cubes
  • 1 teaspoon curry powder
  • 1 teaspoon thyme
  • Salt to taste
  • 1 large onion, divided
  • 4 cups water or stock

Method

  1. Blend the tomatoes, peppers, and half of the onion until smooth.
  2. Heat the oil and fry the remaining onion until golden.
  3. Add the tomato paste and fry for 2 minutes.
  4. Add the blended tomato mixture and fry for 10 minutes.
  5. Add the stock cubes, curry powder, thyme, and salt. Stir and cook for 5 minutes.
  6. Add the rice and stir to coat it evenly with the sauce.
  7. Add the water or stock and bring to a boil.
  8. Reduce the heat to low, cover, and cook for about 20 minutes, checking occasionally until the rice is tender.
  9. Remove from the heat and let it rest for 10 minutes before serving.

Notes

The party smoke: The slightly toasted layer at the bottom is part of what gives party jollof its distinctive flavour. Resist the urge to stir the rice too often while cooking. Be careful not to let it burn excessively.
